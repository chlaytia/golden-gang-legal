Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the GOLDEN GANG Discord bot (“the Bot”), operated by the GOLDEN GANG Team (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when it is used in a Discord server.

1. Information the Bot processes

The Bot currently provides a member-join welcome feature. When a member joins a server where the Bot is installed, the Bot receives event information from Discord and may process:

  • The joining member’s Discord username and user information needed to identify and mention that member in the welcome message.
  • The Discord server and channel information needed to locate the configured #welcome channel and send the message.
  • The join event itself, so the Bot knows when to send the welcome message.

2. How information is used

This information is used only to detect a member joining, identify the member in the welcome message, and send that message to the configured channel. The current Bot code also prints the joining member’s Discord username to the Bot operator’s console for troubleshooting.

3. Storage and sharing

The current version of the Bot does not use a database or intentionally save member information to a separate persistent data store. The username printed for troubleshooting may remain visible in the local terminal or hosting logs according to the operator’s environment and log-retention settings. The Bot sends the welcome message through Discord, so Discord processes that message under Discord’s own policies. We do not sell member information or use it for advertising.

4. Access and permissions

The Bot uses Discord’s bot connection and the permissions and gateway intent configured by its operator. Server administrators decide whether to install the Bot and grant it access to the server and welcome channel. The Bot should only be granted permissions needed for its features.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because the current version does not maintain a separate member database, it has no Bot-managed member profile records to delete. Console or hosting logs may be retained by the operator or hosting provider under their own settings. For questions about those logs, contact the GOLDEN GANG server administrators.

6. Children and Discord

The Bot is provided through Discord and is subject to Discord’s age requirements and platform rules. We do not knowingly ask users to provide personal information directly to the Bot.

7. Changes to this Policy

If we add features that change the information the Bot processes or stores, we will update this Privacy Policy before or when those changes are introduced. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the GOLDEN GANG server administrators through the GOLDEN GANG Discord server.
